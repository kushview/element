Working with Element
====================

The chapters in this part are organised by task rather than by screen. Each
one takes something you want to do, from saving a session to mapping a
controller, and walks through it, referring to :ref:`part-interface` for the
controls involved.

Start with sessions, graphs and nodes, which everything else builds on. The
remaining chapters stand on their own.

:doc:`sessions`
   Creating, opening and saving sessions, autosave and recovery, safe start,
   importing and exporting graphs, and the session settings.

:doc:`graphs`
   Root graphs and subgraphs, their settings, and switching between them with
   hotkeys and MIDI program changes.

:doc:`nodes-and-plugins`
   The life of a node: adding and replacing it, placeholders, enable, bypass
   and mute, processing options, presets and MIDI programs.

:doc:`plugin-manager`
   Scanning for plugins, search paths, favourites and hidden plugins.

:doc:`transport-and-clock`
   Position, tempo and time signature, and MIDI clock in and out.

:doc:`midi-mapping`
   Learning controllers onto parameters, node controls and the transport.

:doc:`virtual-keyboard`
   Playing notes from the mouse or the computer keyboard.

:doc:`osc`
   The OSC host and the OSC Receiver and OSC Sender nodes.

:doc:`preferences`
   Every setting in the preferences window, page by page.
