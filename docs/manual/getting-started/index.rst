Getting Started
===============

|El| is a modular audio plugin host. You build instruments and effects racks
by placing plugins and built-in processors on a graph and wiring them
together, to each other and to your audio and MIDI hardware. This part is
for a first read: it explains what |El| is and which edition you have, gets
it installed and talking to your hardware, builds a first playable session,
and defines the terms the rest of the manual relies on.

Read the chapters in order if you are new to |El|. If you have used it
before, :doc:`concepts` is still worth a look, because the later parts assume
its vocabulary: session, graph, node, port and the active graph.

   What |El| does, the editions it comes in, the supported platforms and

:doc:`installation`

   Sessions, graphs, nodes and ports, presets and programs, devices and the
